Turn a screenshot of a scam, threat, or piece of disinformation into a self-verifying evidence capsule — a single HTML file that proves its own contents haven't changed since you sealed it, openable in any browser, forever, with no account and no server involved.

Anchor this seal publicly (recommended)

A capsule alone can't prove when it was made, or that it wasn't quietly re-sealed later — only that its own contents are internally consistent. Posting this short statement somewhere you don't control (a public reply, a commit, a message to a friend) — before you share the capsule file — timestamps the hash outside your device and closes that gap.

How a capsule protects you

  • 1. Seal Each image is hashed (SHA-256) the moment you add it. Redact anything sensitive first — redaction is destructive and re-hashed, so nothing under a black box survives in the export.
  • 2. Export Everything is bundled into one HTML file: your images, your notes, and a tiny script that re-checks the hashes whenever the file is opened.
  • 3. Anchor Post the short hash statement publicly. This is the one step that actually happens outside your device, and it's what makes the timestamp trustworthy.
  • 4. Share & verify Anyone can open the capsule offline and see a pass/fail banner — no app, login, or lookup required.

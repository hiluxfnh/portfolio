Research

Language models for online safety, built to avoid new harms.

I study how to detect misinformation and hate speech with natural language processing, and how to make that detection fair, hard to fool, and privacy respecting. I want to continue this work in a PhD.

SSRN preprint · 2025

NLP in the Digital Age: Combating Fake News, Hate Speech, and Ethical Risks for Social Integrity

I compared classical and transformer models, LSTM and BERT, for detecting fake news and hate speech, reaching up to 98% accuracy on fake news. I looked closely at the failures that matter in the real world: bias across groups, how easily a filter can be fooled, and privacy. The work is framed against the EU Digital Services Act.
